Files starting with `_` are ignored, so this one is never published. Copy it to
`NN-your-slug.md` (the number only controls ordering between articles that share
a date) and start writing.

Normal markdown works: **bold**, _italic_, [links](https://example.com), lists,
`code`, > quotes, tables, and images from the public folder: `![alt](/logo.png)`.

Inline maths goes between single dollars, like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, and display
equations between double dollars on their own lines:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

Aligned or multi-line equations work too:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0} \\
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} &= \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}
\end{aligned}
$$

A literal dollar sign needs escaping: \$5.
